# Specialist guidance and portable handoffs

Source: [iteration plan](https://chatgpt.com/space/page_b11134b1d1708191adcbfc59518d1580), owner continuation request of 5 October 2026, [merged foundation PR 15](https://github.com/applification/astack/pull/15), and [foundation backlog](../foundation/backlog.md). Base: `400e0c38eb7c11df3a6cbdb41195d90436c39223`.

## Outcome and first slice

Keep `$applification:astack` and its seven delivery routes. Give the lead useful specialist selection/context/authority/proof/reporting guidance and portable text contracts. Make small direct fixes lightweight and COS assignments traceable. Do not implement an outer host, connector, scheduler or durable store in this slice. The foundation remains local-first.

This is a Feature change to engineering guidance. No new visual product flow is involved, so Pencil and Storybook are unnecessary. The site receives explanatory copy and routing examples only. The relevant plan principles are LOOP 01 (intent through proof), WORK 01 (coherent slices), API 01 (validation at an integration trust boundary) and TEST 01 (proportionate evidence); text field conventions are not runtime validation.

## Decisions and next gates

Roles stay references behind the entry; no independent invocation currently needs another skill. Readable `astack-handoff/v1` contracts follow the plan's text-first direction; machine validation is deferred until an actual integration requires it, subject to owner steering. Review these choices against real delivery findings before expanding packaging.

See [milestones](milestones.md) for the next trial and pilot requirements. Static/package checks, examples and a reviewer assessment cannot establish observed agent delivery, installed candidate activation, external dispatch or running-product behavior. Record proof in [evidence](evidence.md) against the tested source identity.
